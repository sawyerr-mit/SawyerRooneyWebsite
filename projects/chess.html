Overview

A Kinova arm and depth camera that read a physical chessboard, follow human moves and respond with moves of their own.

Built during my Maine Space Grant Consortium internship at the University of Maine. A Kinova robotic arm and an Intel depth camera are integrated through ROS 2, MoveIt plans the arm's motion with inverse kinematics, and an OpenCV pipeline calibrates the camera, finds the board corners and maps the geometry onto an 8×8 grid.
