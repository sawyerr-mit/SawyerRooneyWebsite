About

I'm a mechanical engineering student at MIT with a background in computer science, mathematics and physics. I grew up in rural Maine, graduated as valedictorian with a 4.0, and earned an associate degree in computer science from Southern Maine Community College while still in high school.

I'm happiest on problems where theory, code and hardware meet: modeling a gas feed system in Python and then testing the printed nozzle, or pairing electromagnetic simulation with machine learning to design photonic crystals. I'm also curious about lattice QCD and how AI can speed up scientific computing.

Outside the lab I've mentored middle schoolers, helped run community events, taught at a STEM camp, and won a Maine state championship in giant slalom.
