About

Hi, I’m Sawyer. I’m a mechanical engineering student at MIT. I grew up in rural Maine and created this website to document my history of projects and accomplishments. I graduated high school as valedictorian while earning an Associate Degree in Computer Science from Southern Maine Community College, both with a 4.0 unweighted GPA.

Recently, I have shifted more towards 3D modeling, engineering project work, and computational physics. I recently designed the reaction control system thrusters for MIT Rocket Team’s Project Asteria, and have joined the propulsion subteam for MIT's NASA Human Lander Challenge Team. I have also started research on coherent UV generation using Smith-Purcell Radiation.

Outside of STEM, I have spent a lot of time involved in civic engagement outreach programs and civil responsibility initiatives such as: The Can We? Project, middle school student mentorship, National History Day competitions, and the Rho Kappa National Honor Society.

If you are interested in any of my projects or commitments, please check out the rest of this website, and never hesitate to contact me!
